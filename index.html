How booking works

  1. Sign in with your @nift.ac.in email and verify it.
  2. You can browse the seat map and sign in any time. Booking unlocks at the announced time — a countdown is shown on the seat page.
  3. Pick how many tickets you need (max 4 per student), then choose your seats.
  4. Enter a name for every seat you book — each person needs their own name on the ticket to get in.
  5. Confirm. Your seats are held for 5 minutes while you finish.
  6. Your ticket appears under My tickets with a QR code — show it at the gate.

At the gate: carry your NIFT ID. No outside food. No refunds or cancellations.
